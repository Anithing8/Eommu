# Eommu Privacy Policy
_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

Eommu is a task tracker that runs entirely on your device, as an Android app or in your browser.

- **Your data stays on your device.** Tasks, lists, notes and settings are saved in your browser's local storage. There is no account, no server and no database operated by us.
- **No tracking.** Eommu has no analytics, advertising, cookies or third-party scripts, and loads no external fonts or images.
- **Android app.** Data is kept in the app's private storage. The app requests no internet permission. It asks for notification, exact-alarm and start-at-boot permissions only to deliver your reminders.
- **Notifications** are scheduled and shown locally by your device. They only work if you grant permission, which you can withdraw in your browser or system settings.
- **Shared content.** Text or links you send to Eommu through your device's share menu are processed only on your device and saved as a task.
- **Hosting.** The app files are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard request data (such as IP address) under its own [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We do not receive that data.
- **Deleting data.** Delete tasks in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser. Uninstalling the app removes its data on most devices.
- **Children.** Eommu collects no personal information from anyone.
- **Changes.** Updates to this policy will be published in this repository.

Contact: reach us via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Anithing8/Eommu/issues)
